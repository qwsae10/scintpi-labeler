      How to label

      Each event is one satellite at one receiver with S4 above 0.2 for one or more consecutive minutes. Decide what caused it. Full guide: docs/labeling_guide.md in the repository.

      • Scintillation: fast C/N₀ fading on L1 and L2 together, often with σφ and short τ; other satellites in the same part of the sky are affected; no repeat on the neighbouring day.
      • Multipath: low elevation, slow quasi-periodic fading, often stronger on one frequency, little σφ, long τ; the same pattern repeats one sidereal day later (Sidereal repeat tab).
      • RFI: C/N₀ drops on many satellites at the same moment regardless of where they are in the sky.
      • Unsure/Artifact: data gaps, loss of lock, cycle-slip garbage, clock jumps, or you cannot tell.
