      How to label

      Each event is one satellite at one receiver with S4 above 0.2 for one or more consecutive minutes. Decide what caused it. Full guide: docs/labeling_guide.md in the repository.

      • Scintillation: fast C/N₀ fading on L1 and L2 together, often with σφ and short τ; other satellites in the same part of the sky are affected; no repeat on the neighbouring day.
      • Multipath: low elevation, slow quasi-periodic fading, often stronger on one frequency, little σφ, long τ; the same pattern repeats one sidereal day later (Sidereal repeat tab).
      • RFI: C/N₀ drops on many satellites at the same moment regardless of where they are in the sky.
      • Unsure/Artifact: data gaps, loss of lock, cycle-slip garbage, clock jumps, or you cannot tell.

      Calendar

      The calendar in the queue shows the days with events: deeper blue = more left to do, a tick = every event of the day decided. Click a day to work on it alone (in order of receiver and time, unless you pick an order in the filters), ⇧-click a second day for a range, click it again or "All days" to go back to every day. The underlined day is the event on screen.

      Plot

      Swipe sideways with two fingers to pan in time; pinch or ⌘-scroll to zoom at the cursor; ⇧-scroll moves the panel under the cursor up/down and ⌘⇧-scroll stretches it; vertical scrolling scrolls the page. ⌘-drag pans. The time tabs (Overview, 20 Hz, Other satellites, Sidereal) share one time window: zoom on one and the others show the same minutes.

      Drag across the plot to mark part of the event, then press 1–4 for its class; Esc cancels. Click a range to select it: a number changes its class, Delete removes it, its edges can be dragged.

      Labels are saved in the background the moment you press them; the corner of the header shows anything still being written. You can close the browser at any time and continue later.
